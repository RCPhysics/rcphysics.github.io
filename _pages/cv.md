## Research Experience

**Laboratory for Advanced Microscopy & Bioengineering (The LAMB)** — ETSU, 2021–Present
- Founded and leads the LAMB, focused on Raman microspectroscopy, nonlinear optical imaging, and bioengineering
- Secured more than $1.3 million in external funding as PI on a BioMADE grant
- Secured more than $150,000 in gifts and donations to support optics and biomanufacturing research
- Established interdisciplinary collaborations across Biomedical Sciences, Biology, Chemistry, Geosciences, and Education

**Potma Labs — Advancing Nonlinear Vibrational Imaging** — UC Irvine, 2016–2021
*Advisor: Professor Eric O. Potma*
- Developed and demonstrated Third-Order Sum Frequency Generation, a novel nonlinear optical imaging modality with vibrational contrast
- Designed a computer-controlled variable gain photomultiplier tube detection system for multiple imaging modalities
- Characterized lipogenesis in novel mammalian cell types via SHG and SRS microscopy
- Collaborated on NSF, NIH, and DOE grant proposals resulting in more than $1 million in funding
- Evaluated a 3D cell culture model for meibomian gland dysfunction; executed computational design of 10+ Raman and IR probes
- Served as laboratory manager and safety officer from 2017

**Energy Policy Research Group** — UTK & Oak Ridge National Laboratory, 2014–2015
*Advisor: Professor Lee Riedinger, Bredesen Center*
- Thesis on financial and social feasibility of wind energy in West Tennessee; developed plans for net metering and low-income energy efficiency in the TVA region

**Calhoun Laser Spectroscopy Group** — University of Tennessee, Knoxville, 2013–2014
*Advisor: Professor Tessa Calhoun*
- Studied femtosecond pump-probe spectroscopy of drug-membrane interactions; automated lab equipment with LabView

**Soft Matter Biophysics Research Group** — UTK & Oak Ridge National Laboratory, 2012–2013
*Advisor: Professor Alexi Sokolov*
- Studied protein mechanics and characterized secondary structure stiffness via Raman & Brillouin spectroscopy
